# PetRescuePuzzle

 
#Overview.
Pet Rescue Puzzle is a 2D match-3 game (version 1.0). Players match jewels, clear level goals within a move limit, earn stars and score, and rescue pets.



#**Features, described without pointing at code:**

§ Levels with goals, remaining moves, score, and stars

§ Power-ups: grenade, bomb, row, column, laser rifle, bubble gum, laser pistol, snow globe

§ Home hub: play, map, profile, pet collection, daily rewards, lives, coin store, leaderboard, settings, about

§ Lives that refill over time

§ A short story and a world map

§ Flow through loading, home, map, story, and play
